## Usage

Turns matter into lower energy matter, magical waste. Produces a bright flare. Resulting material is instinctually “unclean” or “pure waste”. This residue is 'ripple invisible'[^2.13].

Destroyed magitech devices can leave behind {ASH} residue[^2.13-2].

### Relevant Chains

No known chains.
