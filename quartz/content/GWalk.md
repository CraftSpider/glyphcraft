A program used for creating magitech, [[Diagramming]] and laying out glyph chains for prototyping and production. Apparently in very common use in the glyphcrafting community, including by non-Flamebearers. It can be compared to real-world modeling programs such as blender, as well as PCB CAD software such as KiCAD.

Alongside the its purely theoretical glyph diagramming capabilities, it's capable of integrating lattices with physical parts[^2.3], associating lattices with substrates[^2.3], and generating models for automatically printing out lattice substrates[^2.10].

Ezzen uses the tool frequently, and imagines using it to guide snapweaving[^4.16]. The aren't particularly familiar with any of its production capabilities, only the more theoretical glyph diagramming functions.
