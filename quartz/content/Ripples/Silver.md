> # Silver Ripple
> |  |  |
> | ----- | ----- |
> | Affinity | Time | 

Colored ripple travels forward in time. Silver ripple mirrors this in the opposite direction. Used by Yuuka to read the future. Notably, silver ripple is generated by all colors but the originating color can't be distinguished.

One of the glyphless ripples.
